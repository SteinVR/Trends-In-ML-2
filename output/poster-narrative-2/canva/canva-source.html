github.com/SteinVR/Agentic-RAG-Challenge

01

Hypothesis

Can a Legal RAG pipeline balance answer quality, grounding, valid output, and latency while preserving auditable page provenance?

H1 · System profile. Targets: answer ≥0.90; grounding ≥0.85; 100% valid; every annotated question retains a correct page; latency multiplier ≥0.90.

H2 · Attribution. Select pages after answering to raise macro precision, reduce citation size, and retain a correct page for all 95 annotated questions.

04 · Decision

Engineering rule

Retrieve broadly. Attribute explicitly. Narrow conservatively when support spans pages or documents.

Limitations

  • One saved run; retrospective thresholds; no component ablation.
  • One DIFC benchmark; OCR fallback was implemented but not exercised.
  • Only TTFT was measured - not full latency, throughput, cost, or energy.
