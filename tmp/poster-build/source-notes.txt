Analysis contract:
- Question: Does explicit answer-aware page attribution improve page-level citation precision without causing complete source misses?
- Grain: one benchmark question; 95 questions with non-empty gold page sets.
- Stages: raw retrieval pages, pass-A filtered pages, final answer-aware pages.
- Metrics: macro page precision, macro page recall, F-beta with beta=2.5, pages per question, any-hit count.
- Uncertainty: paired non-parametric bootstrap over questions, 20,000 resamples, deterministic seed 20260903.
- Primary visual: clustered column chart comparing precision, recall, and F-beta across the three stages. Raw is neutral gray, pass A is light blue, final is strong blue; labels and ordering carry distinction in addition to color.
- Caveat: the saved PDFs all expose extractable text, so the experiment does not measure OCR impact.
